# Obsidian Troubleshooting

## Sync conflicts
Close the app on the second device before a big rename.

## Slow search with lots of files
Disable plugins one by one; indexing plugins are the usual suspects.
See [[Obsidian Search (lots of files)]].

## Restricted mode
Community plugins are disabled until you turn restricted mode off.
