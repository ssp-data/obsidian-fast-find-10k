# AI in Data Engineering

How large language models change the daily work of a data engineer: generating SQL,
writing boilerplate pipelines, documenting models and reviewing pull requests.

## Where it helps
- Drafting dbt models and tests from a schema description.
- Explaining legacy stored procedures nobody wants to touch.

## Where it does not
Semantic correctness still needs a human who knows the business.
