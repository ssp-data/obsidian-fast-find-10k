Book notes. The authors define the lifecycle and its undercurrents and stress
that tools come and go while the fundamentals stay.

## Chapter 1
Data engineering is the development, implementation and maintenance of systems
that take in raw data and produce consistent information for downstream use.

## Afterword
The authors expect generative AI to automate parts of the job but not the
judgement about what data is worth keeping. See [[AI in Data Engineering]].
