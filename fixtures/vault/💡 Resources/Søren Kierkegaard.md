# Søren Kierkegaard

Danish philosopher, born in Copenhagen in 1813. His pseudonymous works on anxiety, despair and
the leap of faith shaped existentialism a century later.

## Straße in Berlin

He spent a winter in Berlin attending Schelling's lectures and walking the same Straße every
morning. Æbleskiver from home were missed; a friend from Łódź wrote to him about Œuvre collections.
